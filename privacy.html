Privacy

Phase 1 ORBIT X accounts store your email address, a securely derived password hash, family profile names and avatar choices, and your viewing preferences. Passwords are not stored as plain text.

Sessions use secure HTTP-only cookies. We do not sell account information. Email features will be added only when an email delivery system is configured.

Note: This is an early project privacy page and should be reviewed for your final production launch.
